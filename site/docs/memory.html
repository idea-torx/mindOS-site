Hindsight binding

Hindsight remains the semantic brain through an explicit, provider-neutral binding. SQLite is the single execution authority — Hindsight is never copied into it as a second one. When Hindsight is unreachable, the system degrades honestly: sources are recorded absent rather than guessed at.

Temporal facts

python3 autopilot.py fact-assert --subject service-auth --predicate uses --object postgres-14 --source codex
python3 autopilot.py facts --subject service-auth
python3 autopilot.py search-facts postgres --rank

Facts carry provenance, validity windows, and supersession — evolving ownership, priorities, and decisions stay queryable over time, with BM25 retrieval and temporal hybrid rerank.

Notes with supersession

Notes supersede rather than overwrite: the history of what was believed, when, and by whom is preserved and audited. All free-text fields pass the secret guard.
